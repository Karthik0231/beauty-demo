# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 (with GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, Lucide React, Inter / SF Pro stack)

## Users

Discerning beauty, skincare, and hair clients seeking clinical-grade facials, sculptural bodywork, architectural hair styling, editorial complexion makeup, and private suite appointments.

## Product Purpose

Flagship digital showcase and appointment concierge for **Maison Aurélia**, built strictly in the Apple — Style Reference design language (Obsidian `#000000`, Graphite `#0e0e0e`, Carbon `#1d1d1f`, Steel `#333336`, Slate `#6e6e73`, Ash `#86868b`, Porcelain `#f5f5f7`, White `#ffffff`, Apple Blue `#0071e3`, Electric Link `#2997ff`, Cobalt Link `#0066cc`, Copper New `#b64400`, Optical Yellow `#ffd500`).

## Positioning

A beauty, skincare, hair, and makeup flagship exhibited with the discipline of precision hardware: full-bleed Obsidian campaign stages, Carbon feature workspaces, `28px` media frames, restrained `SF Pro Display`/`SF Pro Text` typography, concentrated Apple Blue booking controls, and a deliberate White pricing/outcomes reset panel.

## Operating Context

Responsive web flagship across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Visitors explore signature beauty campaigns, step through the 4-stage clinical facial protocol, compare rituals and before/after studies, inspect transparent service pricing, and reserve private appointments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Strictly governed by the supplied Apple — Style Reference tokens, typography hierarchy, `28px` media radii, `20px` sticky navigation, `980px` inputs, `170px` Apple Blue pills, and zero drop-shadow elevation.
- Real verified photography assets stored under `public/images/` plus bespoke generated assets when image quota is available.
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger synchronized with Lenis smooth scrolling.

## Brand Commitments

- **Brand Name:** Maison Aurélia
- **Visual System:** Apple — Style Reference (pinned by user, non-negotiable)
- **Iconography:** Lucide React utility glyphs only; zero SVG illustrations.
