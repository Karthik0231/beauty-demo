## Direction Contract

- **THESIS:** Clinical luxury skincare engineered and exhibited like precision hardware — isolating each vessel, serum macro, and phototherapy instrument on an obsidian museum plinth instead of generic pastel beauty carousels or multi-column retail grids.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Pinned Apple — Style Reference system: Obsidian (`#000000`) full-bleed stages, Graphite (`#0e0e0e`) sticky contained navigation (`20px` radius, `1px #282828` keyline), Carbon (`#1d1d1f` / `linear-gradient(#1d1d1f, #000000 288px)`) content and comparison panels, `28px` media/tile geometry with near-zero shadow elevation, Porcelain (`#f5f5f7`) `SF Pro Display` (`Inter` substitute) sculptural headlines (`-1.2px` to `-1.44px` tracking), Ash (`#86868b`) technical telemetry, Copper New (`#b64400`) text-only status markers, Optical Yellow (`#ffd500`) wavelength accents, Apple Blue (`#0071e3`) filled `170px`-radius Buy pills, and a deliberate White (`#ffffff`) comparison reset panel with Cobalt Link (`#0066cc`).
- **STORY:** A visitor immediately encounters Lumina Pro LED & Nocturne Cellular Serum 01 as sculpted optical/clinical objects, scrubs through multi-chapter campaign film & pinned formulation optics, configures regimen variants, compares efficacy metrics side-by-side, inspects botanical/clinical textures, and completes purchase via a persistent Bag drawer.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** `44px` Obsidian global utility bar + `72px` Carbon announcement strip + sticky `20px`-radius Graphite product nav above an uninterrupted `#000000` Obsidian Product Stage where centered photorealistic Lumina Pro & Nocturne 01 hardware dominates the upper canvas and a lower anchor presents the `32px/600` product title, `80px/600` sculptural slogan, `rgba(66,66,69,0.72)` pricing capsule, and Apple Blue `#0071e3` `Buy` pill.
- **FORM:** Apple Midnight Hardware Gallery (pinned by brief), `seed: none (unavailable)` — user-pinned visual world.
